# Privacy Policy — Unlock PDF

**Effective date: June 12, 2026**

Unlock PDF is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Unlock PDF does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and
no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## How your files are handled

All PDF processing happens locally on your device. The PDF you select and the
password you enter are used only in memory on your iPhone to produce the
unlocked copy. They are never uploaded anywhere — the app works fully offline,
including in airplane mode.

The unlocked copy is written to the app's local storage and is only moved
elsewhere when you explicitly save or share it with the iOS share sheet.
